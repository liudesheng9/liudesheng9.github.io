# Debt Overhang

Debt overhang is a phenomenon when the debt overhang distorts entrepreneurs’ future decisions.

### Background

- Entrepreneurs hold equity interests, creditors hold debt interests.

- The investment decision is made by entrepreneurs.
  
- When get the profit, entrepreneurs must prioritize debt repayment.
  
- All the debts are long term.

### Decision-making of a rational entrepreneur

- There is good investment opportunities and bad opportunities.
- If the entrepreneur invest good opportunities, it must repay the debt first and it get a small share of the profit compared with the effort.
- So it will rather invest bad investment opportunities.

Thus debt overhang distorts entrepreneurs’ future decisions.
