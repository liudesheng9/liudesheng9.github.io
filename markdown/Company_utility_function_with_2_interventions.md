## Company utility function with 2 interventions

$$
U_i(a)=a_i\left( b^1_i+\beta\sum_{j \in \mathbb{N}}g^1_{ij}a_j \right)+a_i\left( b^2_i+\beta\sum_{j \in \mathbb{N}}g^2_{ij}a_j \right)-\frac{1}{2}a_i^2
$$

FOC:
$$
a=b^1+b^2+\beta(G^1+G^2)a
$$
Then 
$$
a=[I-\beta(G^1+G^2)]^{-1}(b^1+b^2)
$$
And the social welfare fucntion
$$
W=a^{\top}a=(b^1+b^2)^{\top}[I-\beta(G^1+G^2)]^{-1\top}[I-\beta(G^1+G^2)]^{-1}(b^1+b^2)
$$
Which is in the same form as that in *Target Interventions in Networks*.
