Mathmatical Formula

Inverse of the sum of matrix, Henderson & Searle (1981):

$$ (A+buv')^{-1}=A^{-1}-\frac{b}{1+bv'A^{-1}u}A^{-1}uv'A^{-1} $$

Taylor expansion of Leontief inverse matrix:

$$ \left[ I-\beta G \right]^{-1}=I+\beta G+\beta^2 G^2+\beta^3 G^3+\cdots $$

Rayleigh quotient:

$$ \begin{aligned} R(x)=\frac{x^TAx}{x^Tx}\\ \lambda_1 \geq R(x) \geq \lambda_n \end{aligned} $$
