## Company utility function

$$
U_i(a,G^1,G^2,\omega)=\omega a_i\left( b_i+\beta\sum_{j \in \mathbb{N}}g^1_{ij}a_j \right)+(1-\omega)a_i\left( b_i+\beta\sum_{j \in \mathbb{N}}g^2_{ij}a_j \right)-\frac{1}{2}a_i^2
$$

- FOC:

$$
\vec{a}=\omega\left( \vec{b}+\beta G^1 \vec{a} \right)+(1-\omega)\left( \vec{b}+\beta G^2 \vec{a} \right)
$$

$$
\left[ I-\beta\left(\omega G^1+(1-\omega)G^2 \right) \right] \vec{a}=\vec{b}
$$

$$
\vec{a}^*=\left[ I-\beta\left(\omega G^1+(1-\omega)G^2 \right) \right]^{-1}\vec{b}=A\vec{b}
$$

- We denote $\omega G^1+(1-\omega)G^2$ as $M_{\omega}$ and $\omega^{'} G^1+(1-\omega^{'} )G^2$ as $M_{\omega^{'}}$.
- $\underline{z}=U^{\top}z$

$$
\begin{align}
&\left[ I-\beta\left(\omega G^1+(1-\omega)G^2 \right) \right] \vec{a}=\vec{b}\\
&\Rightarrow\left[ I-\beta M_{\omega} \right]\vec{a}=\vec{b}\\
&\Rightarrow\left[ I-\beta U_{\omega}\Lambda_{\omega}U_{\omega}^{\top}\right]\vec{a}=\vec{b}\\
&\Rightarrow\left[ U_{\omega}^{\top}-\beta U_{\omega}^{\top}U_{\omega}\Lambda_{\omega}U_{\omega}^{\top}\right]\vec{a}=U_{\omega}^{\top}\vec{b}\\
&\Rightarrow\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right] \underline{\vec{a}}=\underline{\vec{b}}\\
&\Rightarrow\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}=\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]^{-1}\underline{\vec{b}}_{\omega}
\end{align}
$$


## Society utility function

$$
\begin{align}
W(\vec{b},G^1,G^2)=\sum_{i \in \mathbb{N}}U_i(a^*,G^1,G^2,\omega^{'})&=\vec{b}^{\top}A\vec{b}+\beta\omega^{'} \vec{a}^{*\top}G^1\vec{a}^*+\beta(1-\omega^{'}) \vec{a}^{*\top}G^2\vec{a}^*-\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}^{*\top}I\vec{a}^*\\&=\vec{b}^{\top}A\vec{b}+\beta\omega^{'}\vec{b}^{\top}A^{\top}G^1A\vec{b}+\beta(1-\omega^{'})\vec{b}^{\top}A^{\top}G^2A\vec{b}-\frac{1}{2}\vec{b}^{\top}A^{\top}A\vec{b}\\
&=\vec{b}^{\top}\left( A+ \beta\omega^{'}A^{\top}G^1A+\beta(1-\omega^{'})A^{\top}G^2A-\frac{1}{2}A^{\top}A\right)\vec{b}\\
&=\vec{b}^{\top}\left( A+ \beta A^{\top}M_{\omega^{'}}A-\frac{1}{2}A^{\top}A\right)\vec{b}
\\&=\vec{b}^{\top}M_W\vec{b}
\end{align}
$$

- Notice that  $\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}=\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]^{-1}\underline{\vec{b}}_{\omega}$
- Notice that  $\vec{b}^{\top}\vec{a}^{*}=\vec{b}^{\top}U_{\omega}U^{\top}_{\omega}\vec{a}^{*}=\underline{\vec{b}}^{\top}_{\omega}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}$
- Notice that $\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}^{*\top}I\vec{a}^*=\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}^{*\top}U_{\omega^{'}}U^{\top}_{\omega^{'}}\vec{a}^*=\frac{1}{2}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}$

$$
\begin{align}
W(\vec{b},G^1,G^2)=\sum_{i \in \mathbb{N}}U(a^*,G^1,G^2,\omega^{'})&=\vec{b}^{\top}A\vec{b}+\beta\omega^{'} \vec{a}^{*\top}G^1\vec{a}^*+\beta(1-\omega^{'}) \vec{a}^{*\top}G^2\vec{a}^*-\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}^{*\top}I\vec{a}^*\\
&=\vec{b}^{\top}\vec{a}^{*}+\beta\vec{a}^{*\top}M_{\omega^{'}}\vec{a}^{*}-\frac{1}{2}\vec{a}^{*\top}I\vec{a}^*\\
&=\underline{\vec{b}}^{\top}_{\omega}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}+\beta\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\Lambda_{\omega^{'}}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}-\frac{1}{2}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}\\
&=\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}+\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\left[ \beta\Lambda_{\omega^{'}}-\frac{1}{2}I \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}
\end{align}
$$

- Compare with original form of $W(\vec{b},G)$, notice that $\omega=\omega^{'}$:

$$
\begin{align}
W(\vec{b},G^1,G^2)&=\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}+\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\left[ \beta\Lambda_{\omega^{'}}-\frac{1}{2}I \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}\\
&=\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}\left[ I-\beta\Lambda+\beta\Lambda-\frac{1}{2}I \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}\\
&=\frac{1}{2}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}
\end{align}
$$





- Optimize target:

$$
\begin{align}
&max\;\; W(\vec{b},G^1,G^2)=\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}+\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\left[ \beta\Lambda_{\omega^{'}}-\frac{1}{2}I \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}\\
&s.t\;\; (\vec{b}-\vec{\hat{b}})^{\top}(\vec{b}-\vec{\hat{b}})=\vec{y}^{\top}\vec{y}\leq C
\end{align}
$$

- Additional Assumption: let $G^1=U\Lambda_{1}U^{\top}$ and $G^2=U\Lambda_{2}U^{\top}$, then $M_{\omega}=\omega U\Lambda_{1}U^{\top}+(1-\omega)U\Lambda_{2}U^{\top}=U\left[ \omega  \Lambda_{1} +(1-\omega)\Lambda_{2}\right]U^{\top}=U \Lambda_{\omega} U^{\top}$ and $M_{\omega}=U \Lambda_{\omega'} U^{\top}$. 

$$
\begin{aligned}
\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega}\left[ I-\beta \Lambda_{\omega} \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega}+\underline{\vec{a}}^{*\top}_{\omega^{'}}\left[ \beta\Lambda_{\omega^{'}}-\frac{1}{2}I \right]\underline{\vec{a}}^{*}_{\omega^{'}}&=\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}\frac{1}{1-\beta\lambda_{\omega i}}\underline{b_{\omega i}^2}+\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}\frac{\beta\lambda_{\omega' i}-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta\lambda_{\omega' i})^2}\underline{b_{\omega' i}^2}=\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}\alpha_{\omega i}\underline{b_{\omega i}^2}+\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}\alpha_{\omega 'i}\underline{b_{\omega i}^2}\\
&=\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}(\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i})\underline{b_{\omega i}^2}
\end{aligned}
$$

Let $x_i=\frac{\underline{y_i}}{\underline{\hat{b}_i}}$, then the optimize target is transformed to
$$
\begin{align}&max\;\; W(\vec{b},G^1,G^2)=\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}(\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i})(1+x_i)^2\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}\\&s.t\;\;\sum_{i=1}^{N}\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}x_i^2 \leq C\end{align}
$$
Then we can derive
$$
L=\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}(\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i})(1+x_i)^2\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}-\mu(\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}x_i^2-C)
$$
Using FOC:
$$
x_i=\frac{(\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i})}{\mu-(\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i})}=\frac{\alpha_{i}}{\mu-\alpha_{i}}
$$
Where $\alpha_{i}=\alpha_{\omega i}+\alpha_{\omega 'i}$,
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}x_i^2=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}\left( \frac{\alpha_{i}}{\mu-\alpha_{i}} \right)^2 = C
$$
Which solves the optimize target.

## The Effect of Deviantation of Network on $y^*$

$$
\Delta_{\lambda_{\omega i}}=\lambda_{\omega' i}-\lambda_{\omega i}=(\omega'-\omega)(\lambda_1-\lambda_2)=\Delta_{\omega}(\lambda_{1i}-\lambda_{2i})=\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}
$$

Then $\alpha_i$ can be written as:
$$
\alpha_i=\frac{1}{1-\beta\lambda_i}+\frac{\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}})-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}))^2}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
&log(\alpha_i)=-log(1-\beta\lambda_i)+log(\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}})-\frac{1}{2})-2log(1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}))\\
&\frac{\partial log(\alpha_i)}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}=\frac{\beta\Delta_{\lambda_i}}{\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_i})-\frac{1}{2}}+2\frac{\beta\Delta_{\lambda_i}}{1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_i})}\\&=2\beta\Delta_{\lambda_i}\left[ \frac{1}{2(\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_i}))-1}+\frac{1}{1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_i})} \right]
\end{aligned}
$$

Suppose $\Delta_{\omega}=0$, then
$$
\frac{\partial log(\alpha_i)}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}=2\beta\Delta_{\lambda_i}\left[ \frac{1}{2\beta\lambda_i-1}+\frac{1}{1-\beta\lambda_i} \right]=\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda_i}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}
$$

Then the effect of changing $\Delta_{\omega}$ depends on $\Delta_{\lambda_i}\lambda_i$ and $(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)$. If $\beta\lambda_i\in [\frac{1}{2},1]$ and $\lambda_i>0$, then the trend of changing $\omega'$ on the $log(\alpha_i)$ is the same as the trend of changing $\omega'$ on the $\lambda_{\omega'}$.

Knowing that $\frac{1}{2}>0$ thus $x_i>0$ because $\alpha_i>0$ and if $x_i<0$, we can simply increase the total utility bu flipping the sign of $x_i$. This also imply that $\mu>\alpha_i\;\forall i$ and if $\beta>0$,  $C\to\infty$, $\mu\to\alpha_1$; if $\beta<0$, $C\to\infty$, $\mu\to\alpha_n$.

$$
log(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}\left( \frac{\alpha_{i}}{\mu-\alpha_{i}} \right)^2)=2\sum_{i=1}^N log(\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}})+2(\sum_{i=1}^N log(\alpha_i)-\sum_{i=1}^N log(\mu-\alpha_i))=log(C)
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial }{\partial \alpha_i}log(\sum_{i=1}^{N}\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}\left( \frac{\alpha_{i}}{\mu-\alpha_{i}} \right)^2)&=0\\
\frac{1}{\alpha_i}+\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_i}-\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}\frac{\partial \mu}{\partial \alpha_i}&=0\\
\frac{\partial \mu}{\partial \alpha_i}=\frac{\frac{1}{\alpha_i}+\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}}\Rightarrow \frac{\partial \mu}{\partial log(\alpha_i)}&=\frac{1+\frac{\alpha_i}{\mu-\alpha_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}}
\end{aligned}
$$

Then
$$
\frac{\partial\mu}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{\partial \mu}{\partial log(\alpha_i)}\frac{\partial log(\alpha_i)}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}=\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[\frac{\frac{\mu}{\mu-\alpha_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}}\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda_i}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}\right]
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}&=\frac{\mu\frac{\partial \alpha_i}{\partial \Delta\omega}-\alpha_i\frac{\partial \mu}{\partial \Delta\omega}}{(\mu-\alpha_i)^2}=\frac{1}{(\mu-\alpha_i)^2}\left( \mu \alpha_i\frac{\partial log(\alpha_i)}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}-\alpha_i \frac{\partial \mu}{\partial \Delta\omega} \right)\\
&=\frac{\alpha_i}{(\mu-\alpha_i)^2}\left( \mu\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda_i}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}- \sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[\frac{\frac{\mu}{\mu-\alpha_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}}\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda_i}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}\right]  \right)
\end{aligned}
$$

Where $\mu$ satisfies
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left( \frac{\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}}}{\mu(1-\beta\lambda)^2-1} \right)^2=C
$$
and
$$
\alpha_i=\frac{1}{(1-\beta\lambda)^2}
$$

## The Effect of Deviation of Network on $W$

We have the optimized result
$$
\alpha_i=\frac{1}{1-\beta\lambda_i}+\frac{\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}})-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}))^2}
$$
For non-deviation result,
$$
\alpha_i=\frac{1}{1-\beta\lambda_i}+\frac{\beta\lambda_i-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^2}=\frac{1}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^2}
$$
To test the effect of adding $\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}$, we will test the effect of changing $\lambda$ in $\frac{\beta\lambda_i-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^2}$. We assume that $\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}=\Delta_{\lambda}$ and $\Delta_{\omega}\Delta_{\lambda_{i}}=\Delta$ for all i. Under this condition, 
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial x_i}{\partial \Delta_{\omega}}&=\frac{\alpha_i}{(\mu-\alpha_i)^2}\left( \mu\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}- \sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[\frac{\frac{\mu}{\mu-\alpha_i}}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\frac{1}{\mu-\alpha_j}}\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}\right]  \right)\\
&=\frac{\alpha_i}{(\mu-\alpha_i)^2}\left( \mu\left( \frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)}-\frac{2\beta^2\Delta_{\lambda}\lambda_i}{(2\beta\lambda_i-1)(1-\beta\lambda_i)} \right) \right)=0
\end{aligned}
$$
Thus $x_i$ won't change, the only thing we need to specify is the change of $\alpha_i$. Let $\Delta=0$:
$$
\frac{\partial }{\partial \Delta}\frac{\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta)-\frac{1}{2}}{(1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta))^2}=\frac{\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta)}{(1-\beta(\lambda_i+\Delta))^3}\beta=\frac{\beta\lambda_i}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^3}\beta=\frac{\beta^2\lambda_i}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^2(1-\beta\lambda_i)}
$$
Thus whether the deviation will increase the social welfare or not will depend on the sign of $\frac{\lambda_i}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)}$.
$$
\frac{\lambda_i}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)}>0\Rightarrow \frac{1}{\lambda_i}>\beta
$$
So if $\frac{1}{\lambda_i}>\beta\;\;\forall i$ increase the deviation $\Delta$ will increase social welfare. This is one of the sufficient but unnecessary condition. Condition
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{\Lambda}\frac{\beta^2\lambda_i}{(1-\beta\lambda_i)^3}\Delta (1+x_i)^2\underline{\hat{b}_{ i}^2}>0
$$
is the necessary condition to let the deviation of network increase the social welfare.

## The dynamic of intervention

Suppose there are infinity of periods. Firms only live 1 periods and social planner lives infinity periods. The total utility of social planner is given by
$$
W_{T}=\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\rho t}W_{t}(b,G^1,G^2)dt=\int_{0}^{\infty}e^{-\rho t}\underline{b}^{\top}_{t}D_{\alpha t}\underline{b}_{t}dt
$$
Where $D_{\alpha t}=diag(\alpha_1,\dots,\alpha_{\Lambda})$.

$b$ is subjected to
$$
||\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t}||^2\leq C
$$
Change the discrete form to continuous form:
$$
||\underline{b}_{t+\Delta}-\underline{b}_{t}||^2\leq C \Delta^2
$$
Take $\Delta\to 0$,
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{\Lambda}\left( \underline{b}_{t+\Delta}-\underline{b}_{t} \right)^2\leq C\Delta^2
$$

$$
\sum_{i=0}^{\Lambda}\left( \frac{\underline{b}_{t+\Delta}-\underline{b}_{t}}{\Delta} \right)^2\leq C
$$

$$
{\frac{d\underline{b}}{dt}}^{\top}\frac{d\underline{b}}{dt}\leq C
$$

Under discrete time, we can establish discrete Bellman equation:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&V(D_{\alpha t}, \underline{b})=\underset{\underline{b'}}{max}\left\{ \underline{b'}^{\top}D_{\alpha t}\underline{b'} + \frac{1}{1+\rho}V(D_{\alpha t},\underline{b'}) \right\}\\
&s.t.\;\;(\underline{b'}-\underline{b})^{\top}(\underline{b'}-\underline{b})\leq C
\end{aligned}
$$

Take derivative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\begin{cases}
&(D_{\alpha t}+D_{\alpha t}^{\top})\underline{b'}+\frac{1}{1+\rho}\frac{\partial V(\underline{b'})}{\partial \underline{b'}}-2\mu (\underline{b'}-\underline{b})=0\\
&(\underline{b'}-\underline{b})^{\top}(\underline{b'}-\underline{b})=C
\end{cases}
\end{aligned}
$$

Then we use $\underline{b}_{t}$ denotes $\underline{b}$ and $\underline{b}_{t+1}$ denotes $\underline{b'}$, let $D_{\alpha t}$ be constant matrix during the period thus it being labeled as $D_{\alpha}$.

$$
\begin{cases}
2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1}+\frac{1}{1+\rho}\frac{\partial V(\underline{b}_{t+1})}{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}-2\mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})=0\\
(\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})^{\top}(\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})=C
\end{cases}
$$

Then
$$
\frac{1}{1+\rho}\frac{\partial V(\underline{b}_{t+1})}{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}} =-2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1}+2\mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})
$$
Then use the Envelope theorem:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{\partial V(\underline{b}_{t})}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}& =\frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1} +\frac{1}{1+\rho} \frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}\frac{\partial V(\underline{b}_{t+1})}{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}\\
& =\frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1} + \frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}\left(-2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1}+2\mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})\right)\\
& =\frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}2\mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})
\end{aligned}
$$
And we can derive

$$
\begin{aligned}
2D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1}&+\frac{2}{1+\rho}\mu_{t+1} (\underline{b}_{t+2}-\underline{b}_{t+1})-2\mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})=0\\
D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1} &= \mu_{t} (\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t}) - \frac{1}{1+\rho}\frac{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t}}\mu_{t+1} (\underline{b}_{t+2}-\underline{b}_{t+1})\\
D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1} &= \mu_{t}\underline{y}_{t}-\frac{1}{1+\rho}\left( I+\frac{\partial \underline{y}_{t+1}}{\partial \underline{b}_{t+1}} \right)\mu_{t+1} \underline{y}_{t+1}
\end{aligned}
$$
For $V(\underline{b}_{t})$, we guess $V(\underline{b}_{t})=\underline{b}_{t}^{\top}D_{V}\underline{b}_{t}$ and $D_{V}=diag\left\{ v_1\dots,v_{\Lambda} \right\}$.
$$
\begin{aligned}
&V(\underline{b}_{t})=\underset{\underline{b}_{t+1}}{max}\left\{ \underline{b}_{t+1}^{\top}D_{\alpha}\underline{b}_{t+1} + \frac{1}{1+\rho}\underline{b}_{t+1}^{\top}D_{V}\underline{b}_{t+1} \right\}\\
&s.t.\;\;(\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})^{\top}(\underline{b}_{t+1}-\underline{b}_{t})\leq C
\end{aligned}
$$
Take derivative:
$$
\begin{aligned}
(D_{\alpha}+D_{V})\underline{b}_{t+1}-\mu_{t} I\underline{b}_{t+1}+\mu_{t} I\underline{b}_{t}=0\\
(D_{\alpha}+D_{V}-\mu_{t} I)\underline{b}_{t+1} = -\mu_{t} I\underline{b}_{t}\\
\underline{b}_{t+1} = -\mu_{t} (D_{\alpha}+D_{V}-\mu_{t} I)^{-1}\underline{b}_{t}
\end{aligned}
$$
And $\mu_{t}$ satisfies the boundary condition:
$$
\sum_{i} \left(\frac{-\alpha_i-v_i}{\alpha_i+v_i-\mu_{t}} \right)^2\underline{b}_{t,i}^2 = C
$$
Then $D_{V}$ satisfies
$$
D_{V} =\mu_{t}^2 (D_{\alpha}+D_{V}-\mu_{t} I)^{-1 \top}(D_{\alpha}+\frac{1}{1+\rho}D_{V})(D_{\alpha}+D_{V}-\mu_{t} I)^{-1}
$$
Which solves the total social welfare function.

## Test the effect on a simple fully connected scene

We will test the game equilibrium in a simple fully connected network and test the network deviation effect.

Set 
$$
G^0=0\;\;G^1=-\phi I + \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N}
$$
Where $\iota_{N}$ denotes a N-dimension vector with value 1 in all entries.

Then the network game equilibrium can be denoted as 
$$
\vec{a}^*=\left[ I-\beta \omega \left(-\phi I + \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N} \right) \right]^{-1}\vec{b}=\left[ (1+\beta \omega\phi)I-\beta \omega \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N} \right]^{-1}\vec{b}
$$
 Given that 
$$
\begin{aligned}
\left[ (1+\beta \omega\phi)I-\beta \omega \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N} \right]^{-1}&=\frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I+\frac{\beta \omega \phi}{1-\beta \omega \phi \iota'_{N} \frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I\iota_{N}}\frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I \iota_{N}\iota'_{N} \frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I\\
&=\frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I+\frac{\beta \omega \phi}{1-\beta \omega \phi  \frac{N}{1+\beta \omega\phi}}\frac{1}{(1+\beta \omega\phi)^2}\iota_{N}\iota'_{N}\\

&=\frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}I+\frac{\beta \omega \phi}{(1+\beta \omega\phi)-\beta \omega \phi  N}\frac{1}{1+\beta \omega\phi}\iota_{N}\iota'_{N}\\

&=\alpha(\omega) I+ \gamma(\omega) \alpha(\omega) \iota_{N}\iota'_{N}
\end{aligned}
$$
Then we can yield the precise formulation of $a$:
$$
a=[\alpha(\omega) I+ \gamma(\omega) \alpha(\omega) \iota_{N}\iota'_{N}]b=\alpha(\omega)b+\gamma(\omega) \alpha(\omega) \sum_{i}b_i
$$
The game equilibrium action is a linear combination of intervention $b$.

Then we can yield the social utility function
$$
\begin{aligned}
W(b)&=a^{\top}\left[ \frac{1}{2}I+(\omega'-\omega)(-\phi I + \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N}) \right]a\\
&=\alpha(\omega)b^{\top}\left[ \frac{1}{2}I+(\omega'-\omega)(-\phi I + \phi \iota_{N}\iota'_{N}) \right]b\alpha(\omega)+\phi N(N-1) (\omega'-\omega)(\gamma(\omega) \alpha(\omega) \sum_{i}b_i)^2\\

&=(\alpha(\omega))^2\sum_{i}b_i^2   (\frac{1}{2} -(\omega'-\omega)\phi)+(\alpha(\omega))^2(\omega'-\omega)\phi(\sum_{i}b_i)^2\\
&+\phi N(N-1) (\omega'-\omega)(\gamma(\omega) \alpha(\omega) \sum_{i}b_i)^2\\

&=b^{\top}\alpha(\omega))^2(\frac{1}{2} -(\omega'-\omega)\phi)Ib+b^{\top}\alpha(\omega)^2(\omega'-\omega)\phi\left[ N(N-1)\gamma(\omega)^2 +1\right]\iota_{N}\iota'_{N}b\\

&=A(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)b^{\top}b+B(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)b^{\top}\iota_{N}\iota'_{N}b
\end{aligned}
$$
We can prove that matrix $A(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)I+B(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)\iota_{N}\iota'_{N}$'s principal eigenvalue is $A+NB$ and has principal eigenvector $(\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}},\dots,\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}})'$.

Known that 
$$
A+NB\geq \frac{b^{\top}(A(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)I+B(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)\iota_{N}\iota'_{N})b}{b^{\top}b}
$$
the inequality takes equal if and only if $b=\lambda (\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}},\dots,\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}})'$.

For resource constrain condition, the optimize target is
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\underset{b}{max}\;\;b^{\top}(A(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)I+B(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)\iota_{N}\iota'_{N})b\\
&s.t.(b-\hat{b})^{\top}(b-\hat{b})\leq C
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
L = b^{\top}(A(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)I+B(\Delta_{\omega},\omega)\iota_{N}\iota'_{N})b-\mu((b-\hat{b})^{\top}(b-\hat{b})- C)
$$
We can yield the result:
$$
\begin{cases}

\end{cases}
$$
which solves the optimized $b$.
